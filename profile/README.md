We are **Lambda Humanoid**, a research team building foundation models for humanoid intelligence. Our goal is to enable robots to learn from people and act across diverse tasks and environments.

Our name reflects this direction: **λ** resembles **人**, the Chinese character for human.

## Research

We study how everyday human experience can become a scalable source of intelligence for robots.

- **Human experience at scale.** Capturing egocentric perception and whole-body interaction in the open world, beyond robot-operated collection.
- **Whole-body intelligence.** Learning policies that coordinate locomotion, posture, and dexterous manipulation through vision and language.
- **Agentic harnesses for humanoids.** Connecting reasoning, memory, and whole-body skills through execution feedback for long-horizon autonomy.

## Selected Work

**λ₀ — Scaling Open-World Egocentric Human Data for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation**  
A whole-body vision–language–action policy that learns through egocentric interaction pretraining, aligned human mid-training, and robot post-training.

**HumanVerse-500 — Whole-body human experience at scale**  
A 500-hour dataset pairing egocentric video with synchronized body and hand motion, built through robot-free capture in everyday environments.
